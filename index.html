About Me

I'm Faren Safari, a graduate accounting student at Cornell's SC Johnson College of Business pursuing my MPS in Accounting with a STEM designation. My path has been anything but linear — I started with a B.S. in Industrial Engineering in Iran, moved into project control for a major construction firm, and ultimately found my calling at the intersection of financial analysis, accounting, and data-driven decision-making.

Before Cornell, I studied at Ithaca College and the University of Missouri Kansas City, earning near-perfect GPAs at both while receiving merit scholarships. I'm drawn to the kind of accounting work that requires real critical thinking — equity valuation, international finance, tax preparation for underserved communities.

Outside the classroom, I'm a student-athlete on the Ithaca College Rowing team, an avid half-marathon runner, an enthusiastic baker, and a player of the Santoor — a Persian hammered dulcimer. I believe the discipline of endurance sport and music translate directly into how I approach complex financial problems.
